01 / 04Why

A hiring pipeline that ships hardware.

Students who finish a flight vehicle have done the thing your job postings ask for: they held a schedule, passed a design review, took a part from CAD to the range, and wrote down what went wrong. That is a short list of people, and sponsorship puts you in the room with them for a year before they graduate.

It is also the cheapest applied-engineering lab in Southwest Florida. Every dollar goes to motors, composite, board fabrication, test equipment and range fees — not overhead.

01

Recruiting access

Resume book, on-site sessions with the team, and first look at graduating members who have documented hardware experience.

02

Brand on the vehicle

Your mark flies. Airframe placement, apparel, launch coverage, and the photography that comes out of every campaign.

03

Association with the first student rocketry program at FGCU, across university channels, local media and outreach events.

04

Technical return

A written flight report with real data, and an open payload interface if you would like to fly something of your own.
