Program / Divisions

Seven
divisions.

One integrated vehicle program, split into the seven places the work actually lives. You join one as your home division and are welcome in any of the others. No prior experience is assumed anywhere on this page.

Divisions
07
Prereqs
None
Div 01Solids · Hybrids

Propulsion

Staffing

Everything that makes thrust, and everything that measures it. For Season 01 that means selecting and characterising the AQ-1 motor, building an instrumented static-fire stand, and writing the propellant handling procedures that keep the rest of it legal and survivable.

What the division owns

  • Motor selection & thrust-curve analysis
  • Static fire stand design
  • Load cell calibration & DAQ
  • Propellant handling procedure
  • Hybrid feed-system research

Skills you will pick up

  • OpenRocket / RASAero
  • LabJack DAQ
  • Hand tools & fixturing
  • NFPA 1127 familiarity
Div 02Composites · FEA

Airframe & Structures

Staffing

The load path from the motor mount to the nose cone. Body tubes, fin cans, bulkheads, centring rings, and the joints between them. If it has to carry a force, this division owns it — from the CAD model through to the part in your hand.

What the division owns

  • Full-vehicle CAD assembly
  • Fin flutter & divergence analysis
  • Carbon / fiberglass layup
  • FEA on flight and landing loads
  • Machining, printing & finishing

Skills you will pick up

  • SolidWorks or Fusion 360
  • ANSYS / FEA basics
  • Composite wet layup
  • Lathe, mill, 3D printing
Div 03PCB · Firmware

Avionics

Staffing

The vehicle's nervous system: sense state, decide, fire charges, log everything. This is where electrical and computing students find the deep end fast, because the code has exactly one chance to run correctly.

What the division owns

  • Flight computer PCB design
  • Embedded firmware & state machine
  • Sensor fusion and filtering
  • Deployment event logic
  • Ground station software

Skills you will pick up

  • KiCad
  • C / C++ on STM32
  • I2C, SPI, UART
  • Python for data reduction
Div 04Dual deploy

Recovery

Staffing

Getting the vehicle back in a condition that lets it fly again. Deceptively deep: descent rate, drift radius, shock cord elongation, charge sizing, and a redundancy argument you have to defend in front of a range safety officer.

What the division owns

  • Descent rate & drift analysis
  • Parachute sizing and fabrication
  • Ejection charge ground testing
  • Shock cord & hardware selection
  • Redundancy failure analysis

Skills you will pick up

  • Sewing & canopy fabrication
  • Black powder handling
  • Statistics of drift
  • Rigging hardware
Div 05TVC · Simulation

Guidance & Control

Recruiting

Today: six-degree-of-freedom simulation to validate everything the other divisions assume. Tomorrow: the Vertex problem — thrust vector control, powered descent guidance, and an autonomous landing. The longest-horizon work on the team.

What the division owns

  • 6-DOF flight simulation
  • Control loop design and tuning
  • TVC gimbal prototyping
  • Kalman filtering / state estimation
  • Powered descent trajectory optimisation

Skills you will pick up

  • MATLAB / Simulink or Python
  • Control theory coursework
  • Numerical optimisation
  • Rapid prototyping
Div 06Science

Payload & Research

Recruiting

A flight without data is a firework. This division gives every launch a reason: instrumented payloads for FGCU research groups, our own atmospheric and vibration data, and the interface documentation that lets outside experiments fly safely.

What the division owns

  • Sensor package design & integration
  • Interface control documentation
  • Vibration & thermal testing
  • Post-flight data reduction
  • Research partnerships across campus

Skills you will pick up

  • Embedded sensing
  • Python / pandas
  • Test planning
  • Technical writing
Div 07Safety · Range · Business

Mission Operations

Staffing

Nothing leaves the ground without this division. Waivers, range coordination, checklists, comms discipline, and the budget that pays for all of it. Also the home for anyone who wants to run sponsorship, media or outreach rather than turn a wrench.

What the division owns

  • FAA waiver & range coordination
  • Launch day checklists & comms
  • Safety review and go/no-go criteria
  • Budget, procurement & sponsorship
  • Outreach, media & documentation

Skills you will pick up

  • Project management
  • Technical writing
  • Graphic design / video
  • Fundraising & outreach
